About Me

I am a Swedish Software Developer currently based in Sydney and relocating to Europe in September 2026. Driven by a lifelong fascination with building digital solutions, I am completing my Bachelor of IT with focus on Mobile App Development this August. With a strong foundation in logic highlighted by a first place win in a schoolwide mathematics competition and a background in media technology, I thrive at the intersection of technical precision and creative problem solving.

I approach coding with the same mindset as my favorite handcrafts embroidery, knitting, and crocheting. These hobbies have instilled in me a deep sense of patience, a sharp eye for detail, and the discipline required to build complex structures one "stitch" at a time. I believe that clean, maintainable code is a craft in itself.

With a focus on mobile app development (React Native & Swift) and a curiosity for emerging tech, my goal is to create meaningful, functional experiences that are as thoughtful in their architecture as they are in their user interface.
